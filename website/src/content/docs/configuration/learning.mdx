---
title: Learning
description: Track recurring quality-gate failures.
---

Learning is opt-in with `sentinel check --learn-after <n>`. Sentinel stores compact metadata in
`.sentinel/learning.json`: the gate and status, a summary of at most 200 characters, occurrence
count, state, timestamps, and whether a prompt has been issued. Full stdout and stderr are never
stored.

An occurrence starts when a gate begins failing and is counted again only after that gate passes.
Skipped or unevaluated gates do not change their records. Once a recurring failure is resolved,
Sentinel asks the agent to add a preventive instruction to `AGENTS.md`; Sentinel never edits that
file. Prompts are issued at most once per record.

To reset learning, delete `.sentinel/learning.json` (or remove the individual record). Decide
whether to commit the file with your project or ignore `.sentinel/` according to your team's
workflow; Sentinel does not modify `.gitignore`.
